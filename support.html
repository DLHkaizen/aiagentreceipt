Support

Answers to the questions people ask most. Anything else: write to us.

How do I add AI Agent Receipt to the Share Sheet?
In any app, tap Share. The first time, swipe the row of apps to the end, tap More, and turn on AI Agent Receipt. Then choose it whenever your AI has done something.
How do I restore Pro?
Settings → Pro → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.
How do I export my data?
Settings → Receipts → Export all receipts (JSON). The file holds every receipt; you can import it again on another iPhone.
What does Free include?
20 new receipts each calendar month and the short statement. When the month's receipts are used, everything you saved stays readable; Pro removes the limit.
Does the app read my messages or watch my AI?
No. It only reads what you choose to share with it, on your iPhone. It cannot see your AI apps and does not record anything by itself.
