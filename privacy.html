Privacy

AI Agent Receipt collects no data. App Store label: Data Not Collected.

  • Everything you save — receipts, originals, notes — is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. There is no account and no server.
  • Pictures and documents you share are read on the device (Apple's Vision and, where available, Apple's on-device model). Nothing is uploaded.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.
